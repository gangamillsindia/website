Our Story

One of the city's most trusted names in flour.

Founded in 1982, Ganga Flour & General Mills began with a simple promise — honest, high-quality flour that families and businesses could rely on. Four decades later, that promise still guides everything we do.

We mill our own flour, which means we control quality from grain to pack. No shortcuts, no compromises — just clean, fresh, consistent produce that has earned a place in thousands of homes and kitchens across the region.

Our flagship Mahaluxmi Bhog Atta has become a household name in Ambala — a symbol of the purity and consistency Ganga Mills stands for.
